CapCut's AI video capabilities can shorten the distance from an idea to a first cut, but a generated result is still a rough edit. Its official tool page presents text- or image-led generation together with captions, voice, and editing; specific models, features, and regional availability can change, so do not treat a button, a free allowance, or one generated result as a lasting promise.

## Treat the AI result as a first cut, not a finished video

Start with a reviewable shot task: who watches, what they should understand, what the image must prove, and what action follows. Generate to validate one shot intention. Do not put a person, product, several transitions, and a full voiceover into one prompt.

```text
audience + single message + subject + one action + setting + camera language + exclusions
```

For example, make a product demo start with one shot of a product changing from still to being picked up, then add title, price, captions, and CTA during editing. If the action is unstable, you know to remake the shot rather than the whole video.

## A reusable four-part process

1. **Input pack**: retain script, source and license for reference images, aspect ratio, brand exclusions, and target platform.
2. **Generated draft**: change one variable per attempt, such as action, framing, or light; retain prompt, tool entry point, and generation time.
3. **Human edit**: inspect pacing, caption breaks, music rights, and whether people or products were replaced incorrectly.
4. **Export acceptance**: play it on the target surface and check first frame, text safe area, audio level, cover, and link.

## Replace “looks good” with acceptance checks

| Check | Pass condition | What to do when it fails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facts | Brand, product, numbers, and voiceover are traceable | Return to the script or asset pack, not wording tweaks |
| Continuity | Subject, clothing, and object remain recognizable between shots | Shorten the shot, use a reference image, or split generation |
| Readability | Captions carry the main message with sound off | Rework copy and safe area instead of adding more effects |
| Publishability | Rights, likeness, platform rules, and CTA are confirmed | Keep it as a draft and repair permissions or assets |
